OFFICE KAYAMA ("we," "us," or "the developer") operates the app Stock Life by Qualinex (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the App.

The App stores the item data you enter (name, photo, quantity, storage location, expiry date, memo, category) only on your device. We do not collect or transmit this data to our servers.

2

Barcode Scanning & Product Lookup

The App's barcode scanning feature looks up the scanned number against the open food database "Open Food Facts" to automatically retrieve a product's name and image. Only the barcode number is transmitted - no name, device identifier, or other personal information is included.

3

"Ask AI for Recipe Ideas" Feature

The App includes an optional "Ask AI for recipe ideas" feature. It uses Android's standard Share function to hand off the food names you select to an app you choose on your own device (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, or any app you have installed).

  • The App itself never transmits this content to any server we operate.
  • You choose the destination app yourself, every time you use this feature.
  • Using this feature is entirely optional, and only the food names you select are shared.
  • How the destination app handles your data is governed by that app's own privacy policy.
How it works This is the same standard Android "Share" mechanism used when sharing a photo or note to another app. The App does not store or automatically choose a destination for what you share.
4

Subscription Payment Information

All features of the App are available through a subscription. Subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment details such as your credit card number are managed by Google and are never collected or stored by us directly. We only receive information confirming whether you have an active subscription.

Please also review Google's Privacy Policy regarding the handling of payment information.

5

Advertising & Analytics

The App does not display advertising and does not use any third-party analytics SDK.

6

Disclosure to Third Parties

Except as described in Sections 2, 3, and 4 above, we do not disclose information obtained through the App to any third party.

7

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any item or category at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device. Note that your subscription is tied to your Google account, so you'll need to cancel it separately through Google Play even after uninstalling the App.
